SMART WATER REDISTRIBUTION — SYNTHETIC DEMO ONLY
City labels: Mumbai, Thane, Kalyan, Dombivli and Navi Mumbai. Map markers use approximate city locations. Water readings, priorities and connections are synthetic, not municipal measurements or verified infrastructure.

UPLOAD STEPS
1. Select Upload CSVs on the website.
2. Consumption history CSV: consumption_with_weather.csv
3. Observation interval: Hourly. Leave cumulative meter readings unchecked.
4. Open Optional supply, zone, connection and weather files.
5. Future supply CSV: supply.csv
6. Zone metadata CSV: zones.csv
7. Transfer connections CSV: connections.csv
8. Leave Historical weather CSV empty: weather is already in consumption_with_weather.csv.
9. Select Generate forecasts. Choose Random forest and a 24, 48 or 72-hour horizon.

FEATURES
- Demand forecast: 90 days of hourly history for 5 demo zones (10,800 rows).
- Weather effect: temperature (Celsius) and rainfall (mm/interval) are embedded in history. Adjust the scenario sliders to compare demand.
- Abnormal consumption: planted high/low usage episodes for historical anomaly backtests. These are not live alerts.
- Shortage prediction: 72 hours of scheduled supply for all 5 zones, with donors and shortage zones.
- Redistribution: 5 directed connections with capacity and cost; zone priority weights guide allocation.
- Network map: approximate city markers; illustrative boundaries.

UNITS AND DATES
Consumption, supply and capacities: cubic metres per hourly interval.
Timestamps: UTC, July 1–September 28, 2026 history; September 29–October 1 supply.
Forecasts start immediately after the supplied history, not today's date.
Season/time/day features are derived from timestamps. No separate season column is needed.
Use all four files together. Extract this ZIP before uploading; the website accepts individual CSVs.
